What it is #

The sound engine that other "sounds pack" mods plug into: goal horns, goal music, period/warmup/game-over music, faceoff and chat-triggered sounds. It discovers .ogg files shipped by sound-pack mods, loads them as Unity AudioClips, plays them synchronised for all players from the server, and stores per-pack and per-user settings. It reads a local donor-preferences file (SteamID → chosen goal song/horn), but nothing is sent anywhere outside the game session.
