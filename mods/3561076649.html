What it is #

A match-statistics mod for Puck: shots, passes, saves, goals, blocks, takeaways, turnovers, possession and star-of-the-game, computed from collisions and game-state events and displayed in the scoreboard/chat. It runs primarily server-side, keeping per-player counters keyed by SteamID, and can serialize a match summary to JSON/CSV on the server disk. It talks to clients only through Unity Netcode named messages.
