What it is #

Replaces the straight stick mesh with a curved/visual variant and adjusts the stick's visual and collision geometry so it behaves plausibly (blade curve, bounce/roll handling, per-player sync). The DLL swaps meshes at spawn and broadcasts curve parameters to other players; the single .unity3d file is the custom stick mesh asset it loads from disk.
